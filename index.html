ⓘ How ads are matched to your org
Sponsored listings are shown based on your org's Salesforce configuration (products, edition, industry, size) and aggregate install patterns — not personal data. Each ad is served by an ISV partner who has defined an audience segment matching your org profile. Partners bid for placement; ads are ranked by relevance score × bid, so higher-quality matches rank higher even at lower bids. You can hover "Why this ad?" on any placement to see the specific match criteria. Manage ad preferences
